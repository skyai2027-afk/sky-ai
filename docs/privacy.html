Sky Publisher – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Sky Publisher is a private automation tool used only by its owner to publish posts to the owner's own social media accounts for "Sky Fiori", a fictional, AI-generated character. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner.

What data we access

The app accesses only the Facebook Page, Instagram account and Threads profile owned by the app owner, for the sole purpose of publishing posts to them. It does not access, collect or store data about any other person, follower or visitor.

What data we store

The app stores the content it publishes (images and captions) and the access tokens needed to publish. Access tokens are kept in encrypted secret storage and are never shared.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Data deletion

Because the app does not collect data from anyone other than its owner, there is no user data to delete. If you believe the app holds any data about you, contact the email below and it will be deleted within 30 days. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Facebook Settings → Business integrations.
